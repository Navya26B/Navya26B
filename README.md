## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏦 [Databricks Insurance ETL — Lakehouse & Star Schema](https://github.com/Navya26B/databricks-insurance-etl-star-schema)
**Stack:** `Databricks` · `Auto Loader` · `Delta Live Tables` · `Unity Catalog` · `Delta Lake` · `Python`

A production-style Medallion (Bronze → Silver → Gold) pipeline that incrementally ingests insurance claims and employee data, applies data quality and validation rules, and models the Gold layer into a dimensional **star schema** (`fact_claims` + `dim_agent`, `dim_customer`, `dim_policy`, `dim_date`) to support fraud-triage reporting for human investigators.

**Stack:** `SQL` · `Python` · `Agile`
